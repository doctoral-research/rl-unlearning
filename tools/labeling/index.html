    How to label

    1. Enter the annotator code you were given, top right. It identifies your labels without your name.
    2. Each task shows one sentence naming a behaviour that someone wants an agent to stop. Mark every step at which that behaviour is happening. Leave the steps where the agent behaves well unmarked.
    3. Judge only from the sentence, the note and the values shown. Where the sentence does not fix a number or a boundary, choose one, write it under Your rule, and apply it the same way across the task. If the behaviour spans several steps, decide which steps carry the label and write that down too.
    4. Look at every step of every episode, then press Mark reviewed. A task is finished when all its episodes are reviewed and your rule is written.
    5. Work alone, and don't discuss the tasks with the other annotators until everyone has finished.

    Your labels save as you go. If the badge at the top says Saved in this browser, press Download my labels when you finish and send the file to the person who asked you to label.
